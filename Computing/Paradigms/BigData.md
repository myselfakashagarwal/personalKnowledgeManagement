Big Data aims for efficiency in processing & managing vast and complex datasets just not big but the datasets which violates the traditional constraints thus require non traditional mechanism and architectures to solve the bottlenecks.  
## Big Data Attributes
### Volume 
As the name sounds the volume refers to sheer amount of data of various types; however this is taken into consideration with respect to data handling capability of system, for example TBs of data for a laptop. 
### Variety 
Different types of data contributing to data set. Apart from type there might be loss of patterns or commonness’s on behalf of which data can be organized. The variety of data includes `structured`, `unstructured` and `semi-structured`.  
### Velocity 
Having world’s time to compute huge data is EG however when needed with a time constraint even with continuity is a real mess. Such as 1TB of data processing per minute.   
### Veracity
It refers to inconsistencies and uncertainty in data, that is data which is available can sometimes get messy and quality and accuracy are difficult to control.
### Value 
At last the goal of all processing handling is getting the value out of it.
